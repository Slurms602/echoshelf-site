Get started

Set up EchoShelf in about 10 minutes

Do the computer first, then the phone. Use the same Google account on both, and your place in a book follows you from one to the other.

⬇ Windows installer ⬇ Android app

1 On your Windows computer

  1. Download the Windows installer (button above) and open it.
  2. If a blue box says Windows protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway. It says this about any app that isn't from a big company. It's safe.
  3. Click Next until you see Finish. EchoShelf opens.
  4. Click ＋ Add / refresh folder and pick the folder that has your audiobooks in it.
  5. Click the ⚙ (top right), then Connect Google account… and your web browser opens.
  6. Choose your Google account and click Continue. If Google says Google hasn't verified this app, click Advanced, then Go to EchoShelf.
  7. Go back to EchoShelf and click Save. Done!

2 On your Android phone

  1. On the phone, open this page and tap Android app (button above) to download it.
  2. Tap the download to open it. If the phone asks, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  3. Tap Install. If Play Protect warns you, tap More details, then Install anyway.
  4. Open EchoShelf and tap Choose audiobook folder. Pick (or create) a folder called Audiobooks, then tap Use this folder and Allow.
  5. Tap the ⚙ (top right), then Connect Google Drive. Pick the same Google account as on your computer and tap Continue. Done!

3 Copy your books to the phone

  1. On the computer, click the 📱 button in EchoShelf (top left, next to ⚙). A square code appears. If Windows asks whether EchoShelf may use the network, tick both boxes and click Allow.
  2. On the phone, tap the download arrow at the top of your library, then Scan QR code, and point the camera at the code.
  3. Tick the books you want and tap Download. Keep the window on the computer open until the phone says Done! It goes straight over your home network, not the internet. Your computer can be plugged in by cable; the phone just needs to be on that router's Wi-Fi. If it gets interrupted, scan again and it carries on where it stopped.
VPN on the computer, or away from home? Send through Google Drive instead: in the 📱 window choose Through Google Drive, tick books and click Upload to Google Drive. On the phone, tap the download arrow, then Check Google Drive. Books are removed from your Drive once they're on the phone.
No Wi-Fi? Use the charging cable instead: plug the phone into the computer, tap the USB notification on the phone and choose File transfer, then copy your book folders into Audiobooks and tap ↻ in EchoShelf.

✓ How it works

  • Pause a book on one device, open EchoShelf on the other, and it jumps to where you stopped.
  • Moved somewhere you didn't want? Tap Undo on the message that appears.
  • Only your place in each book is shared, through a hidden folder in your own Google Drive. Your books, files and other Drive files stay private.

? If something goes wrong

The phone says App not installed or blocks it Open the Play Store, tap your picture → Play Protect → ⚙ → turn off Scan apps. Install EchoShelf, then turn it back on.
The phone says it can't reach the computer In the 📱 window on the computer, click Allow through firewall… and choose Yes, then scan again. Also check the phone is on your home Wi-Fi (the computer can be plugged in by cable). Still stuck? A VPN or an antivirus with its own firewall (Norton, Bitdefender, Kaspersky…) on the computer can block it: turn on its allow local network option, or pause it while sending. Guest or hotel Wi-Fi often blocks this too; use the cable instead.
My place doesn't move to the other device Check both devices use the same Google account and the same files. Open EchoShelf after pausing on the other device. In Settings, Sync now forces a check.
A book is missing on the phone Send it again with the 📱 button on the computer (step 3), or copy its folder into Audiobooks and tap ↻ at the top of the library.
Google says access is blocked Ask the person who sent you EchoShelf. It's a setting on their side.
