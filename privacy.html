Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

The short version: EchoShelf runs on your devices. Its developer operates no servers and collects nothing. If you turn on Google Drive sync, EchoShelf stores a small progress file in a hidden folder in your own Google Drive, which only EchoShelf can access.

This policy covers the EchoShelf audiobook player for Windows and Android (“EchoShelf”, “the app”). It explains what data the app handles, where that data is kept, and how you can remove it.

Data stored on your device

To work as an audiobook player, EchoShelf keeps the following on your device only:

  • your library: the folders you add, book titles, authors, covers, chapters and file locations;
  • listening progress, bookmarks, notes and character notes you create;
  • your settings, and an anonymous random device ID used to tell your devices apart when syncing.

On Windows this lives in %LOCALAPPDATA%\EchoShelf; on Android it lives in the app's private storage. None of it is sent to the developer or to any third party.

Google Drive sync (optional)

Sync is off until you choose Connect and sign in with Google. EchoShelf then requests a single permission:

PermissionWhat it allows
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata Create, read, update and delete EchoShelf's own files in a hidden application data folder in your Google Drive. It does not allow EchoShelf to see, open or change any of your other Drive files.

Each of your devices writes one small file to that folder. It contains:

  • the device's random ID, its name (for example your PC's computer name or phone model, so the app can say “picked up from Pixel”) and platform (Windows or Android);
  • for each book: a fingerprint computed from the audio content, your position, the book's length, whether you finished it, and when it was last updated.

It does not contain book titles, authors, file names, folder paths, covers, bookmarks, notes or any audio.

How this data is used

EchoShelf reads the progress files written by your other devices only to move a book to the position you most recently listened to, and to show you which device it came from. The data is used for nothing else.

Where it is stored and who can see it

The files are stored in your Google account, under Google's own terms and security. They travel directly between your devices and Google over encrypted HTTPS connections. The developer has no access to your Google account, your Drive, or these files, and runs no server that receives them.

Sign-in tokens

After you sign in, Google gives the app a token so it can keep syncing without asking again. On Windows it is stored encrypted for your Windows user account (Windows DPAPI) in %LOCALAPPDATA%\EchoShelf. On Android, sign-in is handled by Google Play services. Tokens never leave your device except when sent to Google to authorize requests.

Sharing, selling and advertising

EchoShelf does not sell, rent, share or transfer your data to anyone. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not use your data to train AI or machine-learning models.

Limited Use disclosure

EchoShelf's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Self-hosted sync (advanced)

EchoShelf also has an optional, advanced setting to sync with a server that you run yourself. If you use it, your progress goes to the server address you entered, and that server's owner (you) is responsible for it. It is off by default.

Deleting your data

  • Stop syncing: in EchoShelf, open Settings and choose Disconnect on each device. This revokes the app's access at Google and deletes the stored token. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete synced progress: open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → EchoShelf → Options → Delete hidden app data.
  • Delete local data: uninstall the app. On Windows, also delete the %LOCALAPPDATA%\EchoShelf folder if you want to remove your library and settings.

Children

EchoShelf is a general-audience app. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Changes will never reduce the protections above without your consent.
