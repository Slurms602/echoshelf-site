Features

Built for people who actually finish their books.

EchoShelf plays the audiobook files you already own: MP3 folders and M4B books. Everything is stored on your device.

Local-first library

Point it at your audiobook folders. Covers, chapters and progress live in a small database on your own machine.

Handoff with undo

The most recent listening wins. A book you're playing is never interrupted, and every jump can be undone.

Five real themes

Not just colour swaps: each theme changes type, shape and texture, from a cream paperback to a glowing terminal.

Every chapter

Reads the full chapter track of big M4B files, so a 500-chapter omnibus shows all 500, not the first 255.

Bookmarks & notes

Mark a moment, jot down who's who, and keep character notes alongside the book they belong to.

Smart rewind & sleep timer

Resume a few seconds back after a break, and fall asleep with a timer that stops at the end of the chapter.

Hotkeys that behave

Global shortcuts and mouse side buttons skip while a book is playing, and leave your browser alone when it isn't.

Mini player

Shrink EchoShelf to a compact player with just the essentials while you work, game or browse.

Android companion

Every feature of the PC app, lock-screen and headset controls, and books copied from your PC over Wi-Fi with one QR scan.

How sync works

Free sync, using storage you already have.

EchoShelf keeps a tiny progress file in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. Only EchoShelf can see it, and it never shows up among your files.

Connect once

Sign in with Google on each device. EchoShelf asks only for its own hidden app folder, not your Drive files.

Listen anywhere

When you pause or close the app, each device publishes where it is: positions and timestamps, never your audio.

Pick up instantly

Open EchoShelf on another device and it jumps to your latest spot, with one tap to undo.

Privacy

Your listening stays yours.

EchoShelf has no servers, no analytics and no ads. The developer never sees your library or your progress.

Read the privacy policy
  • Only the drive.appdata permission: EchoShelf's own hidden folder
  • Synced data is positions and timestamps, never titles, file names or audio
  • Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on your device
  • Disconnect any time, and delete the synced data from Drive in two clicks
